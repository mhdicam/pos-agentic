# Market Research Registry
Status: draft (for review, not signed off)
Date: 2026-11-03
Baseline: Orbis Commerce PRD v1.4.5 + Phase 2.3 handoff
## Hypotheses requiring external validation
- Internet reliability and cash usage make offline POS valuable for some merchants.
- Merchant owner prefers one inventory/finance truth across cashier and online orders.
- Whitelabel storefront and in-house delivery reduce dependence on channel silos.

## Validation plan
Interview retail/F&B merchants across single and multi-outlet; measure transaction mix, internet failure frequency, register/printer ecosystem, courier and COD dispute patterns, buying willingness for subscription/add-ons. Store anonymized source, observation date, sample size and caveats. **No fabricated survey results.**

## Market-sizing method
TAM/SAM/SOM must be built from cited merchant counts and observed price willingness, not guessed in this scaffold.
