## Acceptance Criteria (EARS)
- **RD13-01** (Ubiquitous): The system shall derive the stock card (running balance) and the inventory summary per outlet and period (beginning, purchases, sales, returns, transfer in, transfer out, adjustments, ending, value at cost) from `stockMoves` only, with ending = beginning + Σ columns = on-hand. Tests: `stock card: running balance and type filter`, `summary: columns derive from moves and end = begin + changes; value at cost`
- **RD13-02** (Event): When an owner or manager records an adjustment, the system shall require a reason from the fixed list (Rusak, Kedaluwarsa, Hilang, Pemakaian internal, Sampel, Hitung ulang, Lainnya + note ≥ 5 chars) and write one `ADJUSTMENT` move with its own ref at the session outlet. Tests: `adjust: reason required, other needs note, negative result forbidden, cashier forbidden`
- **RD13-03** (Unwanted): If an adjustment would make on-hand negative, uses a non-integer qty, targets another tenant's SKU, or is made by a role without `stock.adjust`, then the system shall reject it and write nothing. Tests: `adjust: reason required, other needs note, negative result forbidden, cashier forbidden`, `adjust: other tenant sku not found`
- **RD13-04** (Event): When a stok opname is created, the system shall snapshot the system on-hand of every selected SKU (all active SKUs when none selected) at the session outlet; counted qty may change only while DRAFT, and submission requires every line counted. Tests: `opname: snapshot, variance qty/value, approve writes one ADJUSTMENT per variance, replay idempotent`, `opname: rejected writes no moves; cashier cannot create; counts only in DRAFT`
- **RD13-05** (Event): When a SUBMITTED opname is approved, the system shall write one `ADJUSTMENT` per non-zero variance with ref = opname id, where variance = counted − snapshot, applied as a delta on top of current stock; moves recorded after the snapshot (sales, receipts) are therefore preserved, and the UI warns how many such moves exist. Tests: `opname: variance applied as delta on top of moves after snapshot`
- **RD13-06** (Unwanted): If the creator or submitter of an opname tries to approve it, then the system shall reject with `SELF_APPROVAL`; if an APPROVED opname is approved again, then the system shall return it unchanged without new moves or audit. Tests: `opname: snapshot, variance qty/value, approve writes one ADJUSTMENT per variance, replay idempotent`
- **RD13-07** (Event): When an opname is rejected, the system shall require a reason and write no stock moves. Tests: `opname: rejected writes no moves; cashier cannot create; counts only in DRAFT`
- **RD13-08** (Event): When a transfer is sent, the system shall write `TRANSFER_OUT` at the source; when it is received at the destination outlet, the system shall write `TRANSFER_IN` for the received qty (0..sent) and record any shortfall as a discrepancy. Tests: `transfer: send/receive with shortfall discrepancy; balances move between outlets`
- **RD13-09** (Unwanted): If a transfer targets the same outlet, an outlet of another tenant or an inactive outlet, exceeds available stock, is received at a non-destination outlet, is cancelled after SENT, or is attempted by a role without `stock.transfer`, then the system shall reject it. Tests: `transfer: same outlet, other tenant outlet, over available, cashier forbidden, cancel draft`, `transfer: send/receive with shortfall discrepancy; balances move between outlets`
- **RD13-10** (State): While a SKU's available stock at an outlet is ≤ its reorder point (> 0), the system shall list it under "Perlu dipesan" with suggested qty (2 × point − available) usable to prefill a multi-line PO. Tests: `reorder point: needsOrder lists low SKUs with createPo-ready lines`
- **RD13-11** (Event): When an owner rejects a SUBMITTED PO, or a `po.write` role cancels a DRAFT/SUBMITTED/APPROVED PO with no receipts, the system shall require a reason and move the PO to REJECTED/CANCELLED; other states or roles are refused. Tests: `PO reject (owner, SUBMITTED) and cancel (before receipt only)`
- **RD13-12** (Ubiquitous): The demo history shall include an approved opname with variances, a submitted opname, a received and a sent transfer, and reasoned adjustments, without negative stock. Tests: `demo hook: opname approved + submitted, transfers received + sent, adjustments with reasons`, `demoHistory: 30 days via real flows, both outlets, once, rolls back on failure`

- **RD13-13** (Unwanted): If a transfer is sent from a session outlet other than its origin, or to an inactive outlet, then the system shall reject it. Tests: `review fixes: transfer sent only from origin; opname shrink capped, never stuck`
- **RD13-14** (Event): When an approved opname's negative variance exceeds current availability (stock sold after the snapshot), the system shall apply the shrink only down to zero and record the capped lines on the document. Tests: same
## Decisions
- Opname variance rule (RD13-05): apply variance against the snapshot as a delta, not "set to counted at approve time". Reason: counting takes time while the outlet keeps selling; overwriting would erase those sales. Ceiling: a SKU sold during counting and miscounted remains a real variance — a production system may need a count-freeze or per-line timestamps.
- Transfer in-transit stock is not owned by either outlet (left source at SENT, enters destination at RECEIVED); shortfall is recorded, not auto-adjusted.

## Open review gates
Browser-local prototype only; server authorization, concurrency and production integrations are not covered. Avg-cost/landed-cost recalculation, opname print, Excel bulk adjustment and transfer requests from destination are not implemented (benchmark P1).
